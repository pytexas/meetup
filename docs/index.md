## Upcoming PyTexas Meetups

### December Meetup - December 1, 2026

**Securing the AI Stack: From Models to Agents to Infrastructure** - Kriti Faujdar

A practical, layered security framework for production AI systems. This talk covers model-level risks like prompt injection and jailbreaks, agent and application risks, tool and MCP security, and infrastructure concerns such as sandboxing, secrets, and egress. Each layer comes with its threat model and concrete defensive patterns, and attendees leave with a defense-in-depth mental model and a checklist they can apply to their own AI systems.

_Kriti Faujdar is a Senior Product Manager in Agentic Security at Microsoft. She focuses on applying large language models and AI technologies to cybersecurity challenges and building trustworthy AI systems. Prior to her current role, she worked on Azure AI initiatives that helped bring leading generative AI models to developers worldwide. She began her career as a research scientist at ISRO, where she spent seven years building anomaly detection and autonomous operations software for more than 20 satellite missions._
